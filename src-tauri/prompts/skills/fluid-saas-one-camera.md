# Fluid SaaS film: one line, two cursors, one camera

motion_guide {topic:"fluid-saas"} has the beats and the look.

## 1. Measure
analyze_song or analyze_clip_speech: every word's onset. The flown-through word is the line's keyword,
and the fly-through ends on a bar.

## 2. Plan the worlds
Line → cursors → the word grows → fly through it → the next world (the product in use) → the name. Each
world is one ground colour from the brand; the camera never cuts between them.

## 3. Build
1. The line: type-on-voice {text, words} or word-land; a light look.
2. The cursors: two named cursors with avatar tags on arcs from opposite corners; one drag-selects the
   keyword on its onset. Over captured UI, create_product_demo cursors do this; over type, two small
   cursor layers keyed on arcs with the tag following by link.
3. fly-through-word {word, line, next, fill:"accent", pixelate:36}: the next world shows through the
   letters and opens through the counter.
4. The next world: word-land with the keyword in [accent] and the product assembling beside it
   (create_product_demo on a capture, or a line drawing drawn on by trim); camera-match or blur-bridge
   between worlds.
5. logo-lockup or end-card for the name.
6. Take the ground, the accent, the cursor roles and the next worlds from the brand and the product; the
   reference's warm white and magenta are one choice, not the default.

## 4. Look and fix
review_frames: every word readable on its syllable (the reveal 35 ms early), the fly-through sharp before
and after, the cursors never covering the word they act on, the same words never twice on screen (a
caption pill only where the headline is not already saying them).

## 5. Finish
No grain, no vignette, no bloom (launch-light or no finish). sound_the_motion (soft whoosh on the
fly-through, cursor taps, a glass tick on the name); judge_edit; save.
